📖 How to Play Sudoku

🎯 The Goal

Fill every cell so that each row, column, and subgrid box contains each digit exactly once without duplicates:

  • 4×4 Mini: Uses digits 1–4 with 2×2 subgrids.
  • 6×6 Junior: Uses digits 1–6 with 2×3 subgrids.
  • 9×9 Classic: Uses digits 1–9 with 3×3 subgrids.

⚙️ Choosing Grid Size & Difficulty

Before the grid shows, select your desired Grid Size (4×4, 6×6, or 9×9) and Difficulty (Easy, Medium, or Hard), then click ▶️ Start Game. You can return to change settings anytime with the ⚙️ Setup button.

⌨️ Entering Numbers

  • Click a cell to select it, then press a number key on your keyboard.
  • Or click a cell, then tap a number on the on-screen number pad.
  • Press Delete or Backspace (or tap ✕ Erase) to clear a cell.
  • Use Arrow Keys to move between cells.

✏️ Pencil / Notes Mode

  • Toggle with the ✏️ Pencil button or press P.
  • In pencil mode, clicking a digit adds or removes a candidate note in the cell.
  • Notes appear as a small mini-grid of candidate numbers inside the cell.
  • Placing a final number clears all pencil marks in that cell automatically.

🚨 Real-Time Conflict Highlighting & Audio Alert

If you enter a number that causes a duplicate in its row, column, or box:

  • The cell turns red with a dashed border.
  • A bold ! badge appears in the top-right corner (color-blind friendly).
  • A brief two-tone sound alert chimes instantly.
The conflict clears as soon as the duplicate is corrected.

⏱️ Timer & Reset

The timer starts when the puzzle loads. Hit ↺ Reset to clear your entries and restart the timer for the same puzzle. Use ⚙️ Setup to switch grid size or generate a new puzzle.

🎨 Theme Selector

Click 🎨 Theme to choose from 7 animated background themes including Classic, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Galaxy, and Blossom.
